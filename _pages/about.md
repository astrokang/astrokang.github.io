Welcome to my website! 

I am currently a <font color="#CF4C73"><b>PhD student</b></font> working with <a href="http://friendshao.github.io/about/">Prof. Lijing Shao</a> in the <a href="http://kiaa.pku.edu.cn">Kavli Institute for Astronomy and Astrophysics (KIAA)</a> at <a href="https://english.pku.edu.cn/">Peking University</a>.

My research interests center around <font color="#CF4C73"><b>multi-messenger astronomy</b></font> and <font color="#CF4C73"><b>high-energy astrophysics</b></font>, with particular interests in <font color="#CF4C73"><b>compact binary mergers</b></font>, <font color="#CF4C73"><b>relativistic transients</b></font>, and their <font color="#CF4C73"><b>gravitational-wave</b></font> and <font color="#CF4C73"><b>electromagnetic</b></font> signatures. Currently, I mainly focus on how the physical processes associated with compact stars produce observable multimessenger phenomena, bridging theoretical modeling with realistic observations.
